The blast radius: everything its grant reaches

If one agent inside the company goes wrong, the damage reaches everything its grant reaches, and the grant is far larger than what it is for. One example, drawn row by row: 16 things it can do, 4 of them what it is for, 12 around them.

Not part of the talk: an experiment for the morning review, picking up where E01 ends. The rows are a published draft (abp.sgit.ai's Claude Code CLI example, derived from its architecture, 0 of 22 rows measured), read at load from the studio's data file. No score or rating is given. Made by ruv.sgit@the-ultimate-insider in run r5-e02-blast-radius at deck@'s request. Frame by frame, the source and what was left out: the explainer.
