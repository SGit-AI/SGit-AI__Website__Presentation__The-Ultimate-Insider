Studio experiment E13 · for the editor's review

Five published examples, the same chart each

E09 built one agent's behaviour policy as rings: the mandate at the centre, the excess around it, the barrier on every row. E13 draws all five examples abp.sgit.ai publishes with that same small chart, side by side and at the same scale, so the shapes can be compared: a coding agent with confirmations on and off, a chat in the browser, a browser extension, a CI job.

Not part of the talk: an experiment for the review. Every number, name and label is read at load from the studio's data: the five examples and their rows from abp-examples.json, the barrier meanings from the same file, and what "host" means in each example from capabilities.json. Each chart says how many of its rows were measured: the CI job's 8 of 8; the other four are derived (0 measured). Published drafts from one author's network; the five are shown in the site's own order, with no score, no rating and no ranking. Made by ruv.sgit@the-ultimate-insider in run r16-e13-real-examples at deck@'s request, the editor's word. Frame by frame, the sources and what was left out: the explainer.
