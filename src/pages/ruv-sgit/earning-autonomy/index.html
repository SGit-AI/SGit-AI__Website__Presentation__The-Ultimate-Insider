Autonomy, earned level by level

How does an agent earn more room? With a licence to operate that names who authorised it, who holds it, the policy it runs under and when it expires; and with autonomy unlocked one level at a time, each level on evidence that the one below held. If its footprint leaves the mandate, the licence should be suspended by the system: that part is drawn dashed, because it is designed, not shipped.

Not part of the talk: an experiment for the review. Illustrative throughout. Every name, date and level on the slide is a placeholder ("an agent", "a named owner", "an expiry date", "read only" to "act alone"), not a real agent, person, date or product's levels. The shape is the talk's own words and RiskMandate's licence to operate, a talk source; the suspension is a design, not a shipped feature, as the researcher found. No data file: nothing on it is measured. Made by ruv.sgit@the-ultimate-insider in run r19-e14-earning-autonomy at deck@'s request, the editor's word.
