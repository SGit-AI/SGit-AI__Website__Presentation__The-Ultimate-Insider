Studio experiment E12 · the explainer

The blast radius, contained, frame by frame

The one sentence: today no row of this agent's excess is behind a boundary, 12 of 12; a harness has measured that a kernel-held policy refuses a file write and an outbound connect for a child process, and the launcher that would make such a policy a boundary for the agent itself is designed. In that designed state, by the slide's reading, four rows go dark and eight stay lit.

Where every word comes from

  • The ring: the rows of the example claude-code-cli-confirmations-enabled in abp-examples.json, grouped, ordered and numbered by the same code as E02 v3; each row's glyph is its barrier_kind (abp.sgit.ai's glyphs). The counts under the headline and in the boxes are the example's excess (12) and unbounded_excess (12); 8 of 12 is the slide's count of rows its reading leaves lit.
  • The plain labels in the lanes are capabilities.json's.
  • The probes: probes in harness.json; the short names ("Write a file in /tmp", "Connect out to 1.1.1.1:443", "Take 400 MiB under a 128 MiB cap") are the slide's words for each probe's name; "refused" is read from each result; the date from measured_on. A lane is marked "measured in a child" only when its probe's result says it held.
  • The caveats: "a Boundary for the child, a Setting for the session", the launcher above the agent being designed with a first slice since 28 October, fs being "a Setting until the launcher gives it its own uid" and net, runnable as an MVP since 27 October 2026, "a Boundary only for the shell sharness-ai shell starts as root; a Setting otherwise", are the cautions and components of harness.json. "A process cannot bind its own session" restates that caution.
  • Words the slide adds: "today", "designed, not today", "go dark", "stay lit", "still bounded by nothing", "the slide's reading", the lane headers. Each restates a field or labels the slide's own mapping, not a new claim.

Technique

  • One slide on the engine, driven: the ring, lanes and boxes are built once from three data files, then every frame is drawn from the slide's clock by SLIDE_RENDER, so the player can seek, pause and replay it, and ?static shows the end state. The stages are declared in window.RUV_STEPS: 0, 8, 18, 34 and 42 s.
  • E04 v2's language for a path at a wall: every path here stops at a wall that holds, and the wall nudges. A solid wall is a kind measured in a child; a dashed wall is designed only. The designed state itself is drawn in a dashed box, never as today.
  • Three colours of meaning: the agent's red for the excess still lit and the agent's paths, the accent for walls, the designed state and its count, and the neutral grey for a row gone dark (ringed in the accent, solid if measured in a child, dashed if designed). Every text is 20 px or larger on the 1280 by 720 canvas; both themes (?theme=light).
  • It runs only over HTTP. The slide reads its files with fetch when it loads; opened as a file from disk it shows only the headline.

What was tried and left out

  • E02's two columns of labels. The ring is enough for the room to recognise it; only the four rows that change are named, in their lanes.
  • Walls drawn on the ring itself. The rows sit too close to the sector arcs for a path and a wall to fit at 20 px; the lanes carry the walls, and the ring shows only what goes dark.
  • The other three hello probes (the allowed control, the workspace write, the wrong hash): the /tmp write already stands for a write outside the workspace, and the wrong hash closes no row.
  • A score or a percentage. None: a count of rows, today's from the data, the designed one labelled as the slide's reading.
  • Rows 5, 7, 8, 12, 14 and 15 as closed. Each could be argued to a files or network wall; none plainly, so each stays lit, with the reason in the table above.
