Studio experiment E09 · the explainer

Mandate against grant, frame by frame

The one sentence: an Agent Behaviour Policy writes down, for one agent in one deployment, what it is for (the mandate), what it can actually do (the grant), the gap between them (the delta), and what stands in the way of each thing in the gap (the barrier). Part one ended on E02's blast radius; E09 names its rings.

Where every word comes from

  • The definitions are the data file's: the policy, the mandate, the grant and the delta as RiskMandate words them, the enforcer test and the no-rating rule as quoted. The slide adds one line of its own, from the data file's own caution: this mandate is the site's own first draft, not elicited from a deployer.
  • The numbers are the example's fields: mandate 5, aligned 4, grant 16, excess 12, shortfall 1, unbounded excess 12, 0 of 22 rows measured; chat 1, 1, 0. The barrier counts are counted from the rows.
  • Measured in the model, derived here. The source calls the grant measured; this example's rows are derived from what a CLI running as your account is. The slide says both, so nothing derived is shown as measured.
  • The same id means different things per example. The slide shows Claude Code's own reach for "host" (your machine, as your user account), as the researcher's caution asks; the other reaches (your accounts, the internet) are in the table above, not on the slide, for space.
  • 0 of 22. The count of rows measured is of tool-level rows (the 16 capabilities over the tools that reach them), which is why it is 22, not 16; the slide says "tool-level rows".
  • No rating. There is no score, colour of risk or traffic light; the agent colour marks only what is outside the mandate.

Technique

  • One slide on the engine, driven: SVG and list built once from the two data files, then every frame drawn from the slide's clock by SLIDE_RENDER, so the player can seek, pause and replay it, and ?static shows the whole policy.
  • Colour carries one meaning, the agent red, for the excess; the heading colour for the mandate. Every text is 20 px or larger on the 1280 by 720 canvas.
  • It runs only over HTTP. The slide reads both files with fetch when it loads; opened as a file from disk it shows only the headline.

What was tried and left out

  • The chat example as a second picture. A second ring set beside the first left no room for the sixteen rows in plain words; v1 gives the chat example one line and its one-dot shape.
  • The other three examples (confirmations off, the browser extension, the CI runner): left for E13, the small multiples.
  • A word per row on the ring. Sixteen labels at 20 px do not fit round a circle; the dots are numbered and the list carries the words, as in E02 v2.

How it was made

v1 in ruv.sgit run r10-e09-mandate-and-grant: a Ruflo hive with a builder and an editor who checked the slide and this page against the request and the data, and one sgit vault holding the request, each worker's branch and the history. Its row is in the ledger.
