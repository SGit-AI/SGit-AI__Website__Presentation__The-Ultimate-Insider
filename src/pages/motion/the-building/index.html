The building

The one sentence: people who got inside usually turned the wrong way, towards the small things, and agents will not; they go straight to where the value is.

Made by motion@the-ultimate-insider on deck's request of 29 October 2026 (the first of the story animations, from the editor's feedback brief). The stops

Seconds from the start, on the engine's clock (window.RUV_STEPS), so the player starts, stops, steps and seeks it. It ends at 36 s and holds still; with reduced motion it shows the still.

How it was made

  • On the slide engine (talk/engine/slide.js), one 1280 by 720 canvas, self-contained, no script from another host. Every figure walks a polyline timed from the engine's clock, so any frame can be sought.
  • Colours from the talk: the agent red only on the agents; the people who got in in the text colour, not red; the gold in a colour of its own, so it is not read as infrastructure amber. Both themes.
  • Labels in the drawing ("small things", "the gold", "the way in"), one short headline per stop, the ID large, nothing overlapping at the end.
  • Checked at 1280 by 720 and on an iPad viewport (landscape and portrait, touch), in both themes, with the player's clock seeking and pausing it.

Tried and left out

  • A number for how often attackers stopped short: left out, as deck asked; one can be added only from the researcher, through deck.
  • Named incidents on the figures, and anything about how anyone got in: left out on purpose. The door is just a door.
  • The figures walking in the Lemmings manner, one step at a time: left for v2 if deck wants it; v1 glides, which reads better from the back of a room.
