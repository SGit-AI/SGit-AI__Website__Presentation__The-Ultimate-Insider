The studio

Finding better ways to tell the story

The talk has 17 slides that work. The studio is where we look for slides that land: each part of the argument gets a brief, the brief gets storyboards and several versions, the versions are judged side by side, and the best one is promoted into the deck. Nothing here is on stage until it is promoted.

Modelled on the ruv.sgit.ai studio, which built seven versions of one animation and measured the run. This one is wider: one shelf per brief, covering the whole talk, open for as long as the talk is given.

Fourteen experiments, paced one at a time

Part one shows the threat: what an agent is and how it grew, the blast radius of one internal agent, where injected words come from, the over-enthusiastic agent, pivoting, the soft inside. Part two shows the way out, with Agent Behaviour Policies and SHarness and real examples. Made overnight by ruv.sgit from the researcher's data, reviewed by deck every pass, for the editor in the morning. The talk itself is frozen meanwhile.

The loop

  1. Briefdeck writes the job: what the audience must leave with, the material, the sources. Not the picture.
  2. Storyboardruv.sgit sketches the frames: what is on screen at each moment of the beat, and what is said over it.
  3. Versionsseveral builds of the same storyboard, climbing a ladder from plain SVG to the richest technique that earns its place.
  4. Critiqueversions side by side in the player, against the checklist: one idea, every number sourced, readable at the back of the room.
  5. Promotedeck points the beat in talk.json at the winning version. The others stay on the shelf, with why they lost.
  6. RehearseDinis runs it with the words. What does not survive rehearsal goes back to the brief.
