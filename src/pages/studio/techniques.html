The studio

The techniques

A starting toolbox, not a rulebook. The first half is how to draw, carried over from the ruv.sgit.ai studio, where seven versions of one story climbed from a plain SVG diagram to a 3D city. The second half is how to tell: the moves that suit an argument made of three problems and one way out.

How to draw

  • Rung 1 · cheapest

    SVG diagram

    Shapes, lines and type, crisp at any size and easy to change. The engine draws strokes in (data-draw) and fades parts in (data-in). Use first, always: the static version of every idea.

  • Rung 2

    CSS motion

    Transforms and transitions on HTML blocks: cards that arrive, bars that grow (data-grow), numbers that count (data-count). Most slides in the deck today are this rung.

  • Rung 3

    Data-driven drawing

    The picture is computed from a JSON file in SLIDE_RENDER: a timeline placed by date, a bar sized by its number. The slide cannot disagree with its source.

  • Rung 4

    Canvas particles

    Many small moving things: identities, requests, permissions. Good when the point is scale or flow (82 to 1). Costly to make readable; pair with one large label.

  • Rung 5

    2.5D without a library

    Isometric layers drawn with CSS or canvas: the stack of systems an agent moves through, the layers of what stands in the way. Depth without a dependency.

  • Rung 6 · richest

    WebGL

    Three dimensions and real camera moves, as in the ruv.sgit.ai vault city. Only when space itself is the message. Any library is copied into the lab folder: the gate refuses scripts from another host, and a talk venue's network is never to be trusted.

What the stage asks of all of them

  • The room is dark and the screen is far: big type, few words, high contrast. The slide supports the speaker; it is not read.
  • Motion follows the words. Something that moves while Dinis is making a different point steals the room.
  • The livestream is compressed: fine lines and small particles turn to noise, so test a version at a low resolution too.
  • Everything runs offline from the built site, in the player, on one laptop.
