Studio v4: the policy gate

The same 11 actions, twice. Through Ruflo's default gate, all 11 are allowed. Through the ABP in enforce mode, 4 are denied at the gate, 2 wait for a human, 5 pass, and every decision drops a receipt into a hash-chained ledger.

Open it on its own · the end state · 36 seconds, four stops: round 1, the same 11 again, round 2, the ledger.

Where it came from

Version 4 of the seven animations in ruv.sgit.ai's studio, studio/v4/, made in the studio's run 1 and shown at 5:23 in ruv.sgit.ai's talk. Source file: site/studio/v4/index.html in SGit-AI/SGit-AI__Website__Ruv at commit 47b4475.

What changed

  • The clock is this site's slide engine instead of the source's 36-second loop: it plays once and holds round 2's end, and the player pauses, steps and seeks it.
  • A fixed 1280 by 720 canvas (the source fills the window and has a portrait layout); the ID, v4, drawn large.
  • Fewer colours: one per decision (legacy allow in purple, allowed in teal, held in amber, denied in red), the five agents in neutral instead of one colour each, no glow.
  • Every action stays where it ended: denied ones at the gate, allowed ones past it, held ones in the approval bay, so the final frame shows the whole result. In the source they fade out.
  • Larger labels; the decision reason fades once a round has landed, so the end has less text.

Every number, and its source

  • All of it from report.json (POC1, Ruflo 3.56.10): the 11 actions and who made them, each decision in legacy and in enforce mode with its reason, the owner who approves, and both ledgers (length 11, valid).
