ruv.sgit.ai's pieces, on this site's engine

The editor asked that ruv.sgit.ai's animations be reviewed and reused before new ones are made. Each port here is rebuilt on this site's slide engine, so the talk player runs it on its clock: start, stop, two stops per step, seek. Each keeps its Apache-2.0 notice, and every number on it comes from a data file copied unmodified from ruv.sgit.ai.

Which pieces are reused, adapted or left, and where each sits in the talk, is deck@'s call. Until deck's sorting lands, the first two are the ones the editor named, and the third is next in the brief's list.

The ports

  • What an Agent Behaviour Policy is: ruv.sgit.ai's talk slide 7 (run r8-explain). A mandate card, compiled into Ruflo's policy engine, then POC1's 11 actions hit it: 6 of 11 stopped.
  • Studio v4: the policy gate: the same 11 actions through Ruflo's default (all 11 allowed), then through the ABP in enforce mode (4 denied, 2 held for a human), each leaving a hash-chained receipt.
