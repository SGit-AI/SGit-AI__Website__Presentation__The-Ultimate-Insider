How the register of people works (pki.sgit.ai)

A register of people and their public keys, verified both ways. A passkey opens a vault that keeps your keys; you publish the public half, signed; a link between two people counts only when both of them wrote it, which is the answer to the 2019 keyserver flood.

Open it on its own · the end state · 15 seconds, three stops: the page, from a passkey to a message, two ways or not at all.
