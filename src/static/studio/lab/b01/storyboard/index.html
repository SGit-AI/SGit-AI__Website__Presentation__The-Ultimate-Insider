One story, not three: the overlap fills in

Eight frames for brief B01. The three circles keep their colours and their places from the opening to the close. What changes is the space where they overlap: named and empty at the start, filled one colour per act, full and spoken in the collision, then measured and fenced in the way out. The room watches the scenario accumulate instead of being told about it twice.

The idea in one sentence. The scenario is the overlap, and every act adds its own colour to it, so by the collision the audience has watched it fill.
