## How this is made

After each round the conductor runs `python3 admin/tools/night_log.py --mail <its clone of tui-mail-public>`. It reads every wake record (`team/*/wakes/*.json`), the commits each member marked as its own (`@<id>:` or the `Agent:` trailer), the headers of the messages in the public mail vault (never their bodies), and each member's `status.json`; it builds the site and photographs up to four pages that changed in the hour, never a page that shows a key. Each run is kept as `team/conductor/night/<time>.json`, and this article is rewritten from all of them. The protocol behind the verdicts is in [the guide](running-this-agent-team.html).
