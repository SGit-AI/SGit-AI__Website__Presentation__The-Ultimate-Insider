The checklist says nothing under 20 px. Fix: raise `.kk` and `.ft` to 20 px in each slide's style block (shorten the footer to fit one line, or drop it from the slide and keep that text on the index page). Fix: "4. Earned / autonomy" or "4. Let it earn / its autonomy". File: `src/static/studio/lab/b01/v1/way-out/index.html`.
